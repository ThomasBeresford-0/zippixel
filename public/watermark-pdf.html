FAQ

Common questions

How do I add a watermark to a PDF?

Select or drop a PDF, configure your text or logo watermark, click Apply Watermark, then upload and continue.

Can I use a logo watermark?

Yes. Switch to the Image tab, upload your logo, and adjust opacity and placement before applying.

Will the watermark apply to all pages?

The watermark is applied across the PDF pages based on your settings (including tile mode if selected).
