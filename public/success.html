Privacy Policy

Last updated:

ZipPixel is built to do one thing: process your uploaded files and generate a ZIP you can download. We don’t sell personal data and we don’t run ads.

Who we are

ZipPixel (“we”, “us”) is the controller for any personal data processed through this website. Questions? Email support@zippixel.it.com.

What we collect

  • Uploaded files — the files you choose to upload for processing.
  • Basic technical logs — limited request and error information to keep the service reliable and prevent abuse.
  • Payment confirmation — payments are processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation that payment succeeded (we do not store full card details).

How we use information

  • To process your files and generate your ZIP.
  • To provide access to downloads you’ve paid for (including optional share links).
  • To detect abuse, reduce fraud, and keep the service stable.

Legal basis

  • Contract — to provide the service you requested (processing files and providing downloads after payment).
  • Legitimate interests — to protect ZipPixel from abuse, maintain reliability, and improve service stability (minimal logging only).

How your files are handled

  • Your uploaded files are stored temporarily so we can create your ZIP.
  • Files and generated data are automatically deleted after a short period (typically within around 1 hour).
  • Once deleted, downloads and share links will no longer work — so download promptly after purchase.
  • We do not use your files for advertising, and we do not use them to train models.

Share links

If you choose the optional shareable download link, anyone with that link can download the ZIP while it’s available. Treat share links like a password and only share with people you trust.

Cookies

ZipPixel does not use tracking cookies for advertising. We may use essential cookies only when needed to run the service.

Third parties

We use trusted providers to operate ZipPixel:

  • Stripe for payments.
  • Hosting / infrastructure providers to run the site and process uploads.

These providers process data only to deliver the service and according to their own privacy and security terms.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data during upload and processing. No online service can guarantee 100% security, but ZipPixel is intentionally minimal to reduce risk.

Your choices

  • You can choose not to upload files.
  • You can close the tab and return later, but files are temporary and may be deleted automatically.
  • If you believe we’ve retained something unexpectedly, contact support and we’ll investigate.

Contact

Questions? Email support@zippixel.it.com.
