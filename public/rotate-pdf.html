    FAQ

    Common questions

    Can I rotate only some pages?

    Yes. Rotate any page from the preview. Your downloaded PDF includes only your chosen rotations.

    What angles are supported?

    90°, 180°, and 270° (in 90° increments).

    Will rotation change the content?

    No. Rotation changes page orientation only — the page content remains the same.

    Do I need an account?

    No signup required.
