    Rotate PDF pages online

    Rotate scanned pages, sideways exports, or misaligned documents without installing software. Preview your pages, rotate only what you need, then download the updated PDF.

    Common uses

    • Fix sideways scan pages
    • Rotate a single page in a multi-page PDF

    FAQ

    Can I rotate only some pages?
    Yes — preview a page and rotate it. Your downloaded PDF reflects your chosen rotations.
    What rotation angles are supported?
    90°, 180°, and 270° (in 90° steps).
    Do I need an account?
    No signup required. Upload, rotate, continue, then download.
    Will rotation change the PDF contents?
    Rotation updates page orientation. The page content remains the same.
