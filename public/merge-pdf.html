    FAQ

    Common questions

    Can I reorder pages before merging?

    Yes — drag pages in the grid to the exact order you want. The merged PDF follows that order.

    Will any pages be dropped?

    No. If previews are limited for speed, all remaining pages are still included in the merge.

    Do I need an account?

    No signup required. Upload, continue, then download.

    Is there a file limit?

    Limits are enforced in the app. If you hit a limit, split the job into smaller batches.
