    A CV that looks fine on your computer can still be too large for a job application website. Many portals set strict PDF upload limits, which causes frustrating errors right before submission. This page is built to help you make your CV PDF smaller so it is easier to upload to recruiter systems, NHS Jobs, Indeed and other application forms.

    Common CV upload limits

    Many job sites and application systems reject CV PDFs that are too large. Exact limits vary, but these are common ranges:

    • Indeed and recruiter portals: often around 2MB
    • Workday-style application systems: often 2MB to 5MB
    • NHS Jobs and similar public-sector forms: can have strict upload caps
    • Email attachments: commonly around 10MB to 25MB total

    If your CV PDF is above the allowed size, it may fail to upload even if the document itself looks fine.
