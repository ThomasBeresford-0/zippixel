FAQ

Common questions

How do I password protect a PDF?

Select or drop a PDF, set and confirm a password, click Encrypt PDF, then upload and continue.

Can you recover my password if I forget it?

No — protected PDFs can’t be unlocked without the correct password. Store it safely.

Will protection change the PDF content?

Password protection restricts access but should not change layout or readability of your document.

Is my PDF secure?

Files are processed to generate your protected output. See Privacy for retention and deletion details.
