    FAQ

    Quick answers.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Upload → checkout → download. That’s it.

    Are my files private?

    Yes. Files are used only to generate your ZIP and are deleted automatically after a short time.

    What formats do you support?

    Most common image formats (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC/HEIF, AVIF).

    Is it free?

    It’s a one-time payment to cover processing + hosting. You’ll see the price at checkout.

    What if the ZIP fails?

    If your ZIP doesn’t generate or you can’t download it, email support and we’ll refund you.
