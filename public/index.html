    FAQ

    Quick answers. No nonsense.

    Do I need an account?
    No. ZipPixel runs without accounts — upload, process, download.
    Can I compress a PDF to a smaller size?
    Yes — choose “Compress PDF”, upload one PDF, pick a compression level, and ZipPixel will reduce file size for easier email and uploads.
    What files can I convert?
    Currently: JPG, PNG, WebP, and PDF. Use “Auto detect” if you’re not sure.
    Can I merge PDFs?
    Yes — choose “Merge PDFs”, upload 2+ PDF files, and ZipPixel will combine them into a single PDF.
    Can I split a PDF?
    Yes — choose “Split PDF”, upload a PDF, and ZipPixel will split it into separate pages for download.
    How many files can I upload?
    Up to 50 files per job.
    Is checkout secure?
    Yes — payments are handled by Stripe Checkout.
    What happens to my files?
    They’re processed for your job so you can download the result. See the Privacy page for details.
