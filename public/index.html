    FAQ

    How do I compress a PDF?
    Click Select PDF (or drag & drop), then click Upload, then Continue. After checkout, download your compressed PDF.
    Is PDFOperations secure?
    Files are uploaded to temporary storage to process your request. We aim to delete uploads automatically over time. Don’t upload files you don’t have the right to process.
    Do I need an account?
    No. There are no accounts or subscriptions. You pay once when your download is ready.
    What are the limits?
    Max 50 files per job and max 25MB per file. Compress PDF uses 1 file.
