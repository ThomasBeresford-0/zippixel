    FAQ

    Quick answers. No nonsense.

    Do I need an account?
    No. ZipPixel is pay-once with instant access after checkout.
    What files can I convert?
    Currently: JPG, PNG, WebP, and PDF. Use “Auto detect” if you’re not sure.
    Can I merge PDFs?
    Yes — choose “Merge PDFs”, upload 2+ PDF files, and ZipPixel will combine them into a single PDF.
    How many files can I upload?
    Up to 50 files per job (Pro supports up to 50). Standard supports up to 10.
    Is checkout secure?
    Yes — payments are handled by Stripe Checkout.
    What happens to my files?
    They’re processed for your job so you can download the result. See the Privacy page for details.
