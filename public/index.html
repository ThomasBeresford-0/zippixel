    Make a PDF smaller (without messing it up)

    Practical guidance — not waffle.

    If your PDF is too large to upload or email, compression is usually the fastest fix. For most documents (forms, invoices, contracts), Balanced reduces size while keeping text sharp.

    If you’re submitting to strict systems (2MB / 5MB / 10MB), switch to Maximum and re-check the output size. For scanned/image-heavy PDFs, start with Light first to preserve detail.

    Need other tools? Use Merge, Split, Convert or Rotate.

    Will compression change how my PDF looks?
    Balanced keeps layout and readability for most documents. Maximum is more aggressive and may reduce image detail to hit strict size limits.
    Which compression level should I choose?
    Balanced for everyday PDFs. Light if quality matters most. Maximum for strict portals and upload caps.
