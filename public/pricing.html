Pricing FAQs

Do you charge a subscription?
No. You pay once per job, only when your download is ready.
How do tiers work?
Standard is £2.99 for up to 10 files. Pro is £9.99 for up to 50 files. Your tool shows the tier before Stripe Checkout.
What is the Share link add-on?
It adds +£2.49 and creates a download link instead of sending attachments.
What do I download?
It depends on the tool. Some outputs are a single PDF (merge/rotate/compress), and others may bundle into a ZIP (split and multi-file conversions).
Do you store my files?
Files are stored temporarily to process your request and deliver the download. See the Privacy page for retention details.
