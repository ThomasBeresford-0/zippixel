How billing works

Simple, predictable

Pricing is based on how many files are in your job. The tool picks the correct tier and you confirm the total at checkout.

What counts as a “file”?

A file is one item you upload into the tool (for example: one PDF or one image). Some tools output one file; others output a ZIP when there are multiple results.

When do I pay?

Only when your download is ready and you choose to proceed. Stripe shows the full total before you confirm payment.

Pricing FAQs

Straight answers
Do you charge a subscription?
No. It’s pay-once per job. You only pay when your output is ready to download.
How do tiers work?
Standard is £2.99 for up to 10 files. Pro is £9.99 for up to 50 files. The tool selects the tier automatically and Stripe shows the total before you confirm payment.
What is the Share link add-on?
Share link adds +£2.49 and generates a download link so you can send one link instead of attachments.
What do I download?
It depends on the tool: Compress / Merge / Rotate produce a single updated PDF. Split produces a ZIP of per-page PDFs. Convert outputs one file per input (bundled into a ZIP when needed).
Do you store my files?
Files are stored temporarily to process your request and deliver the download. See Privacy for details on retention and handling.
