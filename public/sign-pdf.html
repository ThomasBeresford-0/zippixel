    FAQ

    Common questions

    How do I sign a PDF?

    Select or drop a PDF, create a signature (type, draw, or upload), place it on the correct page, apply it, then upload and continue to download.

    Can I sign on the correct page?

    Yes. Use the page controls to navigate, then place your signature precisely before applying.

    Do I need an account?

    No signup required. Apply your signature, upload, then download.

    Is my PDF stored?

    Signing is applied locally to create a new signed PDF. See the Privacy page for retention and deletion details.
