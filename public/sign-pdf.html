    FAQ

    Common questions

    How do I sign a PDF?

    Select or drop a PDF, create a signature by typing, drawing, or uploading, place it on the correct page, apply it, then continue to secure download.

    Can I sign on the correct page?

    Yes. Use the page controls to navigate, then place your signature precisely before applying it.

    Do I need an account?

    No signup required. Apply the signature, then continue once the signed PDF is ready.

    Is my PDF stored?

    Signing is applied locally to create a new signed PDF. See the Privacy page for retention and deletion details.
