    FAQ

    Common questions

    Does this guarantee under 2MB?

    It targets 2MB using maximum compression. Text-heavy PDFs usually compress well. Scanned or image-heavy PDFs may not always reach 2MB without lowering image quality.

    What if my PDF is still over 2MB?

    Try exporting the original PDF at a lower image resolution, remove large images if possible, then compress again.

    Is this the same as the free homepage compressor?

    No. The homepage is a general free compressor. This page is for a specific 2MB upload limit and uses maximum compression automatically.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Upload your PDF, prepare the smaller version, then unlock the download.
