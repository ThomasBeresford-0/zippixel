Privacy, by design
ZipPixel is built to process uploads and generate a ZIP. No ads. No data selling. Minimal logs only.

This Privacy Policy explains what ZipPixel collects, why we collect it, and how long we keep it. If you have questions, email support@zippixel.it.com.

Who we are

ZipPixel (“we”, “us”) is the controller for personal data processed through this website. Contact: support@zippixel.it.com.

What we collect

  • Uploaded files — the files you choose to upload for processing.
  • Job identifiers — IDs used to connect uploads, payment confirmation, and downloads.
  • Share-link data (optional) — a token, expiry time, and basic counters to operate shareable links.
  • Basic technical logs — limited request and error information to keep the service reliable and prevent abuse.
  • Payment confirmation — payments are processed by Stripe; we receive confirmation that payment succeeded (we do not store full card details).

How we use information

  • To process your files and generate your ZIP.
  • To provide access to downloads you’ve paid for (including the optional share link).
  • To detect abuse, reduce fraud, and maintain service reliability.

Legal basis

  • Contract — to provide the service you requested (processing files and delivering a ZIP after payment).
  • Legitimate interests — to protect ZipPixel from abuse and keep the service stable (minimal logging only).

File storage and retention

  • Your files are stored only as long as needed to generate your ZIP and provide access to the download you purchased.
  • ZipPixel is designed for temporary storage. You should download promptly after purchase.
  • Retention can vary depending on operational needs (for example: reliability, abuse prevention, failed processing, or abandoned sessions). We avoid keeping files longer than necessary, but we do not guarantee an exact deletion time.
  • We do not use your files for advertising, and we do not use them to train models.

Share links

If you choose the optional shareable download link, anyone with the link can download the ZIP while it remains available. Treat the link like a password and only share it with people you trust.

  • Share links are time-limited (currently up to 7 days from purchase).
  • If files are deleted or the link expires, the share link will stop working.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card details. Stripe may collect and process personal data according to their privacy policy.

Cookies

ZipPixel does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We may use essential cookies or local storage where needed to operate the service (for example, basic functionality and session flow).

Third parties

ZipPixel uses trusted providers to operate the service, including:

  • Stripe for payments.
  • Hosting / infrastructure providers to run the site and store files temporarily.

These providers process data only to deliver the service and according to their own privacy and security terms.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data during upload and processing. No online service can guarantee 100% security, but ZipPixel is intentionally minimal to reduce risk.

Your choices

  • You can choose not to upload files.
  • Files and links are temporary — download promptly after purchase.
  • If you believe something has been retained unexpectedly, contact support and we’ll investigate.

Contact

Questions? Email support@zippixel.it.com.
