Privacy Policy

Last updated:

ZipPixel is built to do one thing: compress your files and generate a ZIP you can download. We don’t sell personal data and we don’t run ads.

What we collect

  • Uploaded files — only for processing your ZIP.
  • Basic technical logs — to keep the service reliable and prevent abuse (e.g., errors and request metadata).

How your files are handled

  • Your files are stored temporarily to create your ZIP.
  • Files are automatically deleted after a short time.
  • We do not use your files for training models or marketing.

Cookies

ZipPixel does not use tracking cookies for advertising. If we use essential cookies (for example, to maintain a session during processing), they’re only used to run the service.

Third parties

We may use trusted infrastructure providers to host and run ZipPixel. These providers process data only to deliver the service.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data during upload and processing. No internet service can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we keep the product minimal by design.
