Privacy Policy

Last updated:

ZipPixel is built to do one thing: compress your images and generate a ZIP you can download. We don’t sell personal data and we don’t run ads.

What we collect

  • Uploaded images — only for processing your ZIP.
  • Basic technical logs — to keep the service reliable and prevent abuse (for example, errors and request metadata).

How your files are handled

  • Your files are stored temporarily to create your ZIP.
  • Files are automatically deleted after a short time.
  • We do not use your images for training models or marketing.

Cookies

ZipPixel does not use tracking cookies for advertising. If we use essential cookies (for example, to maintain a session during processing), they’re only used to run the service.

Third parties

We may use trusted infrastructure providers to host and run ZipPixel. These providers process data only to deliver the service.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data during upload and processing. No internet service can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we aim to keep the product safe and minimal by design.

Contact

Questions? Email support@zippixel.it.com.
