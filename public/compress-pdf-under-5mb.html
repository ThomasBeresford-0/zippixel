    FAQ

    Common questions

    Does this guarantee under 5MB?

    This page applies maximum compression automatically to target 5MB limits. Most text-heavy PDFs compress well. Some scanned/image-heavy PDFs may not reach 5MB without reducing readability.

    What if my PDF is still over 5MB?

    If it’s a scan or image-heavy document, try exporting at 150–200 DPI, or removing large images before exporting to PDF, then compress again.
