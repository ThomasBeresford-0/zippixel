    FAQ

    Common questions

    How do I compress a PDF to under 5MB?

    Select your PDF, click Upload, then Continue to download the compressed file. If you still exceed 5MB, compress again or export your PDF with lower image resolution.

    What if the portal limit is 10MB or 25MB?

    This page targets strict 5MB limits, but it works for higher caps too. If you only need under 25MB (email), you can compress less aggressively and keep more detail.

    Will compression reduce quality?

    Text-heavy PDFs typically compress with minimal visible change. Image-heavy PDFs may lose detail depending on the original file.

    Is my PDF secure?

    Files are processed to generate your output. See the Privacy page for retention and deletion details.
